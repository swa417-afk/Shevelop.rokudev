# Roku Channel Store Submission Guide

## Overview
This guide provides essential information and steps required for submitting your application to the Roku Channel Store.

## Pre-submission Checklist
1. Ensure your Roku application meets the [Roku Developer Guidelines](https://developer.roku.com/docs/developer-program/dev-guide/developing-apps/faq.dev)
2. Prepare your application assets (icons, screenshots, etc.)
3. Complete the metadata required for submission.

## Submission Steps
1. Access the Roku Developer Dashboard.
2. Select your application from the list of applications.
3. Fill in all the required fields, including:
   - Title
   - Description
   - Category
   - Launch URL
4. Upload the required assets.
5. Review and confirm your submission details.
6. Click on the ‘Submit’ button to finalize your submission.

## Post-submission
- Wait for feedback from the Roku review team.
- Respond to any requested changes in a timely manner.
